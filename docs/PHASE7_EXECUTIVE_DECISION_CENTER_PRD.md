# Phase 7 — Executive Decision Center (Part 1)

## Product Requirements Document

### Vision
Transform AEDIP from a data platform into a **mission control system for decision-makers**. The Executive Decision Center is the first screen every leader opens each morning. It answers five questions instantly: What happened? Why? What needs attention? What is likely next? What should I do?

### Problem Statement
Executives across African enterprises currently waste hours every morning opening spreadsheets, dashboards, reports, emails, and WhatsApp messages to understand the state of their organization. Information is fragmented, late, un-actionable, and often lacks context. The Decision Center solves this by aggregating all organizational signals into a single, intelligent, action-oriented interface.

### Objectives
- Reduce decision-making time by at least 60%.
- Reduce manual report preparation by at least 70%.
- Improve visibility across departments and industries.
- Improve accountability by linking insights to owners and deadlines.
- Increase productivity through prioritized, explainable AI recommendations.
- Reduce operational risk with early warnings and forecasts.
- Provide one source of truth grounded in ETL, AI, and organizational data.

### Success Metrics
- Average time from login to first actionable insight under 30 seconds.
- 90% of daily active users opening the Decision Center as first action.
- 80% of recommendations accepted or acted upon within due date.
- Alert resolution rate above 75% within SLA.
- Page load time under 2 seconds on standard networks.

### Scope (Phase 7 Part 1)
This document defines requirements, architecture, and design. Implementation (Part 2) will follow in the next phase and will integrate with existing authentication, ETL, AI platform, and dashboard modules without redesign.

## 2. Complete User Journey

### Morning Ritual — The Executive
1. **Login / Landing**: User logs into AEDIP and lands automatically on the Decision Center.
2. **Welcome Panel**: Sees personalized greeting, today’s date, organization, role, today’s tasks, upcoming meetings, and unread notifications.
3. **Health Score Glance**: Views the Organization Health Score™ and immediately sees the biggest category dragging the score down.
4. **AI Daily Briefing**: Reads a plain-English summary of the last 24 hours generated by AI.
5. **Critical Alerts**: Reviews prioritized alerts, expands one to see impact, recommended action, owner, and due date.
6. **Decision Feed**: Scrolls an intelligent feed of events and clicks an item to open the relevant module.
7. **Executive KPIs**: Scans key numbers and trends for the organization.
8. **Forecast Panel**: Reviews AI forecast for the next 30/90 days with risks and opportunities.
9. **Pending Actions**: Takes action on approvals, reports, workflows, and ETL jobs.
10. **AI Recommendations**: Reviews ranked recommendations and assigns an owner.
11. **Quick Actions**: Imports data, builds a dashboard, runs ETL, or generates an AI summary.

### Alert Response Journey — Operations Manager
1. Sees a high-priority alert: "Revenue declined 12% in Eastern Region."
2. Clicks the alert.
3. Decision Center opens the relevant drill-down: regional sales dashboard, anomaly details, and AI explanation.
4. Reads recommended action: "Review discounting policy and follow up with regional manager."
5. Assigns owner and due date.
6. Marks alert as in-progress; Decision Center tracks resolution.

### Cross-Industry Journey — Hospital Director
1. Lands on Decision Center.
2. Organization Health Score shows "Patient Care" and "Medicine Stock" categories.
3. AI Daily Briefing: "Bed occupancy exceeded 85%. Three medicines below reorder level."
4. Executive KPIs show Admissions, Discharges, Surgeries, Revenue, Staff on Duty.
5. Forecast panel predicts patient admission surge next week.
6. Quick Action: "Generate bed allocation report."

## 3. Functional Requirements

### FR-1 Welcome Panel
- Display personalized greeting using the user’s first name.
- Show current date and time in the user’s timezone.
- Show active organization and role.
- Show today’s task count and link to task module.
- Show upcoming meetings (next 3) and unread notification count.

### FR-2 Organization Health Score™
- Compute a composite score from 0 to 100.
- Categories: Data Quality, Reporting, Security, Automation, User Activity, System Performance, KPI Performance, Compliance.
- Each category contributes a weighted sub-score.
- Show trend versus last month.
- Generate recommendations to improve the lowest categories.
- Allow drill-down into category details.

### FR-3 AI Daily Briefing
- Automatically generate a briefing every morning or on-demand.
- Include insights from sales, attendance, inventory, ETL, reports, approvals, data quality, and security.
- Plain English, 3 to 7 bullets.
- Link each bullet to its source module or drill-down.

### FR-4 Critical Alerts
- Classify alerts as High, Medium, or Low priority.
- Alert types: revenue decline, stock low, attendance drop, fraud indicator, data quality issue, security event, failed ETL, late report.
- Each alert includes: severity, department, impact, recommended action, owner, due date, status.
- Support filtering by severity, department, and status.
- Support assigning owner and due date.
- Track alert lifecycle: new → in-progress → resolved → dismissed.

### FR-5 Decision Feed™
- Activity feed aggregating events from ETL, reports, AI insights, alerts, user actions, and system events.
- Each feed item has: title, timestamp, category, status, and link to relevant module.
- Support infinite scroll or pagination.
- Support filtering by category and date range.
- Feed items must be actionable (e.g., "Open", "Resolve", "Assign").

### FR-6 Department Performance
- Show a card or row per department.
- Each department shows: status indicator, health score, 3 to 5 KPIs, trend, and top recommendation.
- Support department selector in top navigation.
- Default departments are configured per organization.

### FR-7 Executive KPIs
- Display 6 to 12 KPIs based on the organization’s industry.
- KPIs must include: current value, target, trend, status (on-track / warning / critical).
- Support custom KPIs configured by admins.
- KPIs update automatically when underlying ETL or AI engines run.

### FR-8 Forecast Panel
- Display forecast chart for selected KPI.
- Show confidence interval and trend direction.
- List expected risks and opportunities.
- Allow selecting forecast horizon (30, 60, 90, 365 days).

### FR-9 Pending Actions
- Aggregate approvals, reports, workflows, ETL jobs, data validation tasks, and manual tasks.
- Group by type and priority.
- Each action has title, owner, due date, and direct link.
- Support marking actions as done or delegating.

### FR-10 AI Recommendations
- Rank recommendations by value: High, Medium, Low.
- Each recommendation explains: why, expected benefit, estimated impact, and recommended owner.
- Support accept, reject, schedule, and delegate actions.
- Learn from user feedback to improve ranking.

### FR-11 Upcoming Reports
- Show calendar of upcoming reports: monthly, quarterly, annual, compliance, government, department.
- Include due date, owner, status, and link to generate or submit.

### FR-12 Quick Actions
- Provide one-click actions: Import Data, Build Dashboard, Create Report, Run ETL, Create Workflow, Generate AI Summary, Invite User, Create Department.
- Each action opens the relevant module pre-filled with context.

### FR-13 Recent Activity
- Show recent reports, ETL runs, users, dashboards, alerts, and AI actions.
- Timestamps and actor names.
- Link each item to its detail view.

### FR-14 Industry Support
- Support Health, Education, Agriculture, Church, NGO, Retail, Government, Manufacturing, Business, Finance, Insurance, Tourism, Mining, Logistics.
- Each industry maps to a default KPI set and health categories.
- Framework is shared; only configuration and widgets differ.

### FR-15 Top Navigation
- Global search across modules, reports, alerts, and AI insights.
- Organization selector.
- Department selector.
- Date filter (Today, This Week, This Month, This Quarter, Custom).
- Notification center.
- AI assistant quick chat.
- Quick actions dropdown.
- User profile.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-1 Performance
- First meaningful paint under 1.5 seconds on 3G networks.
- Full Decision Center load under 2.5 seconds on broadband.
- API response time for Decision Center data under 500ms (p95).
- Real-time widgets update without full page reload.

### NFR-2 Scalability
- Support 10 to 10,000 concurrent users per organization.
- Support multi-tenant data isolation.
- Cache computed health scores and daily briefings.
- Background workers for AI generation and heavy aggregations.

### NFR-3 Reliability
- 99.9% uptime target for core Decision Center APIs.
- Graceful degradation if AI service is unavailable.
- Fallback to cached data when real-time sources fail.

### NFR-4 Security
- Enforce RBAC on every widget and action.
- No sensitive data exposed in client bundle.
- Audit log every Decision Center action.
- Input sanitization on search and filters.

### NFR-5 Maintainability
- Modular widget architecture.
- Industry configurations stored in database, not code.
- Clear separation between data services, business logic, and UI components.
- Comprehensive tests for each widget and API.

### NFR-6 Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance.
- Keyboard navigable.
- Screen reader friendly.
- Color-blind safe indicators (icons + text + color).

### NFR-7 Localization
- Support English, French, Portuguese, Swahili, Arabic, and local languages.
- Date, number, and currency formatting per locale.
- RTL layout support for Arabic.

### NFR-8 Data Freshness
- Health score and KPIs updated at configurable intervals (default: every hour).
- Alerts generated in near real-time.
- Daily briefing generated once per day or on-demand.

## 11. Accessibility Requirements

- **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** compliance for all Decision Center components.
- **Keyboard navigation**: every widget reachable with Tab; actions with Enter/Space.
- **Screen readers**: semantic HTML, ARIA labels, live regions for alerts and briefing updates.
- **Focus management**: visible focus rings, logical tab order, focus trap in modals.
- **Color contrast**: minimum 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for UI components.
- **Color-blind support**: status indicators use icons + text, not color alone.
- **Reduced motion**: respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for animations.
- **Readable typography**: minimum 16px body text, scalable to 200% without horizontal scroll.
- **Alternative text**: all charts have text summaries and data tables.
- **ARIA live regions**: alert counts and daily briefing updates announced.

## 12. Performance Requirements

- **Time to First Byte (TTFB)** for Decision Center page: under 200ms.
- **First Contentful Paint (FCP)**: under 1 second on 4G.
- **Largest Contentful Paint (LCP)**: under 2 seconds on broadband.
- **Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS)**: below 0.1.
- **API response time**: p95 under 500ms for aggregated endpoint.
- **Widget load**: each widget independently loaded; failure in one does not block others.
- **Polling**: 60 seconds for feed and alerts; hourly for health score and KPIs.
- **Server-Sent Events**: optional push for high-priority alerts.
- **Bundle size**: initial JS bundle under 250KB gzipped.
- **Image optimization**: SVG icons preferred; WebP for any images.

## 14. Dark Mode Strategy

### Design Tokens
- Background: `bg-background` (light) / `bg-background-dark` (dark).
- Surface: `bg-surface` / `bg-surface-dark`.
- Text: `text-primary` / `text-primary-dark`.
- Border: `border-default` / `border-default-dark`.
- Accent: consistent primary brand color with adjusted opacity.

### Mode Implementation
- CSS variables or Tailwind `dark:` variants.
- Toggle in user profile and system preference detection.
- Persistent preference in local storage and user profile.

### Chart Adaptations
- Charts switch to dark palette (light grid lines, white text, muted colors).
- Plotly and Recharts themes defined for both modes.

### Accessibility in Dark Mode
- Maintain contrast ratios.
- Avoid pure black backgrounds; use dark gray surfaces.
- Status colors remain distinguishable.

## Integration with Existing AEDIP

The Executive Decision Center will be built as a new module without changing existing phases:

- **Phase 1/2 (Architecture & MySQL)**: reuse `shared/database.py`, multi-tenant schemas, and repository pattern.
- **Phase 3 (Auth & RBAC)**: reuse JWT auth, `get_current_user`, `require_permissions`, and role system.
- **Phase 4 (ETL Engine)**: consume ETL job status, data quality results, and pipeline outputs.
- **Phase 5 (AI Platform)**: consume AI insights, forecasts, anomaly alerts, KPI recommendations, and daily briefing generation.
- **Phase 6 (Dashboard)**: reuse data service patterns and extend with Decision Center widgets.

New files to be created (Part 2 implementation):
- `decision_center/` package in backend.
- `frontend/app/decision-center/` and related components.
- Alembic migration for Decision Center tables.
