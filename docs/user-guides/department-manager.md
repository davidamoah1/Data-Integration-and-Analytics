## Purpose

Guide for Department Managers (dept_manager role).

## Scope

Department-level operations and team oversight.

## Audience

Department managers.

---

## 1. Overview

As a Department Manager, you oversee operations within your department, view department analytics, and coordinate team activities.

## 2. Key Capabilities

- View department dashboards and reports
- Manage ETL pipelines and workflows
- View org-level analytics
- Manage datasets
- Export data and reports

## 3. Dashboard

- View department-specific dashboards
- Monitor KPIs and metrics
- Drill down into data details

## 4. Data Management

- Upload datasets for your department
- View and manage existing datasets
- Create ETL pipelines for data transformation
- Export data via ETL

## 5. Reports

- Generate reports from department data
- Export reports (PDF, CSV, Excel)
- Schedule recurring reports
