# DataFlow — Product Overview

## The Problem

African businesses generate massive amounts of data daily — sales records, patient files, student grades, farm yields, NGO beneficiary lists. But most lack the technical teams and expensive tools needed to turn that data into decisions.

**The result?** Decisions made on gut feeling. Opportunities missed. Resources wasted.

## The Solution

**DataFlow** is Africa's first AI-powered data intelligence platform. Upload any spreadsheet and get:

1. **Automatic column mapping** — AI recognizes "rev" as "revenue", "dt" as "date"
2. **Instant KPIs** — Revenue, growth rates, averages, trends — computed automatically
3. **Beautiful dashboards** — Dark-themed, interactive, exportable
4. **AI insights** — Anomaly detection, forecasting, natural language queries
5. **Africa-aware** — Understands GHS, NGN, KES, ZAR, local regions and industries

## Target Markets

### Primary: African SMEs (50-500 employees)
- Retail chains tracking sales across regions
- Hospitals managing patient data and billing
- Schools monitoring student performance
- NGOs reporting to donors
- Agricultural co-ops tracking yields

### Secondary: African Enterprises (500+ employees)
- Banks needing compliance reporting
- Telecom companies analyzing churn
- Government agencies managing programs
- Manufacturing companies optimizing supply chains

### Tertiary: International organizations working in Africa
- Development agencies (USAID, DFID, UN)
- Consulting firms (Big 4 in Africa)
- Multinational corporations with African operations
