## Purpose

Product vision and mission statement.

## Scope

The strategic direction of the DataFlow platform.

## Audience

All stakeholders.

---

## 1. Mission

DataFlow empowers organizations to transform raw data into actionable intelligence through an integrated platform that combines ETL, analytics, AI, and document capture in a single, multi-tenant SaaS environment.

## 2. Vision

To be the definitive enterprise data intelligence platform where organizations across all industries — healthcare, education, business, and research — can upload, analyze, visualize, and report on their data without needing separate tools for each step.

## 3. Core Values

1. **Data-First**: Every feature starts with the user's data
2. **Enterprise-Grade**: Security, multi-tenancy, and compliance by design
3. **Industry-Aware**: Studios tailored to specific industry needs
4. **AI-Augmented**: AI assists, not replaces, human intelligence
5. **Blank-Slate Onboarding**: Users start clean, no demo data pollution

## 4. Target Market

- Healthcare organizations needing patient analytics
- Educational institutions tracking student performance
- Businesses requiring sales and operational analytics
- Research teams conducting statistical analysis
- Government agencies needing data governance

## 5. Competitive Advantage

- Multi-tenant architecture with strict org isolation
- Industry-specific studios (not generic dashboards)
- Smart Data Capture with OCR and confidence scoring
- AI-powered analytics assistant
- Invitation-based enterprise onboarding
- Comprehensive audit logging for compliance
