# AEDIP End User Guide

## Welcome to DataFlow

DataFlow is your Enterprise Data Intelligence Platform. This guide helps you get started in minutes.

## Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### 1. Log In

- Navigate to your DataFlow dashboard URL
- Enter your username and password
- Contact your administrator if you don't have credentials

### 2. Choose Your Data Source

- **Live Database**: Connect directly to your organization's database
- **Upload File**: Upload a CSV or Excel file for quick analysis

### 3. Explore Your Dashboard

- **KPI Cards**: See key metrics at the top
- **Charts**: Scroll down to view revenue trends, category breakdowns, and regional analysis
- **Filters**: Use the sidebar to filter by region, category, or date range

### 4. Ask the AI Copilot

- Scroll to the **AI Copilot** section at the bottom
- Type a question in plain English (e.g., "What are the top selling products?")
- The AI will analyze your data and provide insights

### 5. Export Your Data

- Click **Download Filtered Data** in the sidebar
- Get a CSV file with your current filtered view

## Features

### Dashboards

- Interactive charts with real-time data
- KPI cards showing key business metrics
- Filterable by region, category, and date range
- Export to CSV

### AI Copilot

Ask questions like:
- "Show me profit by region"
- "What's the revenue trend over the last 6 months?"
- "Which products have the highest profit margin?"
- "Explain the sales performance"

### ETL Pipelines

- Automated data extraction and loading
- Schedule recurring pipelines
- Monitor job status and history
- Data quality checks

### Industry Templates

Choose from pre-built packs:
- **SME Pack**: Sales, inventory, customers, profit, cash flow
- **Education Pack**: Students, attendance, exams, fees
- **Healthcare Pack**: Patients, lab, pharmacy, bed occupancy
- **Church Pack**: Membership, attendance, giving, outreach
- **Government Pack**: Revenue, budget, projects, procurement
- **NGO Pack**: Donors, projects, beneficiaries, grants

## Tips

- Use filters to focus on specific data segments
- Export data regularly for offline analysis
- Ask the AI Copilot for quick insights
- Check the quick-start checklist in the sidebar
- Contact your admin for new data sources or pipelines

## FAQ

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Contact your administrator.

**Q: Can I upload my own data?**
A: Yes, use the "Upload File" option in the sidebar.

**Q: Why is my dashboard empty?**
A: The database may not have data yet. Ask your admin to run an ETL pipeline.

**Q: How accurate is the AI Copilot?**
A: The AI analyzes your actual data. Always verify critical decisions with manual review.
