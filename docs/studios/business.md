## Purpose

## 1. Overview

The Business Studio provides analytics for business operations, including sales, finance, operations, and customer insights.

## 2. Use Cases

- Sales performance analysis
- Revenue trend tracking
- Customer segmentation
- Operational efficiency
- Financial forecasting

## 3. Data Types

- Sales records
- Customer data
- Financial transactions
- Inventory data
- Operational metrics

## 4. Access

- Route: `/studios` → Business card
- Permission: `dashboard.view` (minimum)
- Org-scoped: All data isolated by organization
