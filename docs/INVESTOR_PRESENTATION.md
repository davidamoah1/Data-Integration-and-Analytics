## Slide 2: The Problem

### African Businesses Are Drowning in Data, Starving for Insights

- **70% of African SMEs** make decisions without data analytics
- **No affordable tools** — Power BI costs $10/user/mo, Tableau $35/user/mo
- **Foreign tools don't understand African data** — currencies, regions, industries
- **No data engineers** — most companies can't afford technical teams
- **Data sovereignty concerns** — cloud-only solutions don't work for sensitive data

**The result:** Billions in lost productivity, missed opportunities, and poor decisions.

## Slide 3: The Solution

### DataFlow: Upload Any Spreadsheet → Get Insights in Minutes

**4-step process:**
1. Upload CSV/Excel

4. Share, export, and decide

**No data engineer required. No configuration. No training.**
